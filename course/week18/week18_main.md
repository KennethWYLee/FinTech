# Week 18 — Advanced Issues and Research Questions

Week 18 connects the semester's work with advanced issues in portfolio weighting. Depending on limitations observed in the final reports, the class may examine time-varying weights, market-regime changes, online portfolio selection, machine-learning approaches to portfolio allocation, distributionally robust optimization, estimation uncertainty, and transaction-cost-aware rebalancing.

Students use evidence from the semester to show how an empirical limitation can motivate a research question, a formal method, and an appropriate validation design.
