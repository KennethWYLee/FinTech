# Week 10 — Individual Short Reports

Every student gives a concise explanation of the midterm quantitative-trading work, including students whose implementation was completed as a team. Each report explains one important empirical result, one problem found in the data, backtest, rebalancing process, or performance evaluation, and one question about converting trading information into portfolio weights.

The reports provide the evidence used to introduce the relationship among trading signals, expected returns, risk estimates, and portfolio allocation. Presentation order and time limits will be announced in class.
