# Week 16 — Final Check

Before identifying the frozen Git commit, confirm that:

- the report, presentation, code, and saved results describe the same method and version;
- the data, dates, information boundary, baselines, costs, constraints, and rebalancing rule are stated;
- the proposed method and required baselines are compared under the same announced conditions;
- the out-of-sample results can be reproduced from the submitted materials;
- every main conclusion cites a result, and unfavorable or failed cases are not omitted;
- additional analyses are clearly separated from the common comparison; and
- generative-AI use is disclosed according to the course policy.

For the oral defense, prepare to explain one weight calculation, one reported result, and one limitation without relying only on the presentation slides.

If an error is found after the freeze, retain the original commit and state what was corrected and whether the correction changes the conclusion.
