# Week 16 — Final Reports, Session 1

The first set of teams presents its portfolio-weighting method, financial motivation, mathematical definition, implementation, baseline comparison, out-of-sample results, and limitations. Class time is reserved for questions about assumptions, data, estimation windows, rebalancing, transaction costs, portfolio concentration, and interpretation.

Teams presenting in Week 17 may attend and ask questions but may not revise the work submitted before Week 16.
