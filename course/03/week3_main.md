# Week 3 — Information Timing, Features, and Signals

Week 3 focuses on point-in-time information, financial features, and simple trading signals. Students distinguish observation, publication, signal, decision, execution, and evaluation times. They examine look-ahead bias and information leakage, apply appropriate lags, and translate financial hypotheses into measurable features and simple rule-based signals.

By the end of the week, students should be able to state when each variable becomes available and verify that a feature does not use unavailable future information.
