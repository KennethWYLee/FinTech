# Week 10 — Support

## Check before identifying the frozen version

- The report, presentation, and code describe the same question, data period, strategy, and results.
- Dates, frequencies, units, return definitions, and information timing are stated where they affect interpretation.
- The prediction model and baseline use the same target dates, and trading strategies use compatible execution and cost assumptions.
- Predictions, signals, positions, trades, weights, turnover, costs, and net returns connect without missing steps.
- Tables and figures identify the strategy, benchmark, period, metric, unit, and cost treatment needed to interpret them.
- Conclusions distinguish what the results show from what remains uncertain or untested.
- The frozen Git commit opens correctly and contains the material needed to reproduce the reported results.

## Compare the other teams

For each team, identify the strongest traceable result, the most important unresolved limitation, and whether the solution is comparable under the common data, timing, execution, cost, and evaluation conditions. Use those observations to explain the final ordering. If the evidence does not support a distinction between two teams, report that limitation instead of inventing one.

If a correction is made after the frozen version, keep the original commit available and explain which results and conclusions change. Follow the full frozen-version rule in the [course syllabus](../README.md).
