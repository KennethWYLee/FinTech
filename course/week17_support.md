Before finalizing the ranking, check that:

- every team except your own appears once;
- each position cites a reported result, implementation detail, or oral answer;
- differences caused by data, dates, costs, constraints, or evaluation procedures are identified;
- missing evidence is not treated as favorable evidence; and
- the explanation states what the available evidence cannot establish.
