# Week 9 — Asynchronous Midterm Submission

Week 9 is conducted asynchronously during the instructor's travel period and the university midterm examination week. Teams submit reproducible quantitative-trading code, results, and an English report through the LMS. The submission documents the financial question, data, information boundary, features, trading rules, backtesting procedure, rolling or expanding windows, rebalancing assumptions, transaction costs, benchmarks, performance results, and limitations.

Teams may not modify the submitted strategy after the designated deadline. LMS submission links and deadlines will be provided through the university system and are not stored in this public repository.
