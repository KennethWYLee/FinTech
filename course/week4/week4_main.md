# Week 4 — Concept Report and Basic Backtesting

Week 4 begins with the first concept report. Each team briefly presents its financial question, proposed data, target, information boundary, benchmark, and planned trading approach. The remaining class introduces the construction of a basic backtest. Students convert signals into positions, trades, holdings, and portfolio returns and examine entry and exit rules, holding periods, position limits, cash positions, long-only and long-short strategies, and execution-price assumptions.

By the end of the week, students should be able to implement a reproducible backtest and explain every transformation from a signal to a reported return.
