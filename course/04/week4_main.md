# Week 4 — Basic Backtesting

Week 4 introduces the construction of a basic backtest. Students convert signals into positions, trades, holdings, and portfolio returns. The class examines entry and exit rules, holding periods, position limits, cash positions, long-only and long-short strategies, and the relationship between signal timing and assumed execution prices.

By the end of the week, students should be able to implement a reproducible backtest and explain every transformation from a signal to a reported return.
